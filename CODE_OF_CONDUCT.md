# Community conduct

Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Welcome participants regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability. Critique ideas and code without personal attacks.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, unwanted sexual attention, deliberate disruption, and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable in project spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate contributions and temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Enforcement should be proportionate and explained privately when possible. Conflicts of interest should be disclosed and handled by another available maintainer.

Report concerns to the repository owner's contact channel listed on their GitHub profile: https://github.com/alexmckenley. Do not post sensitive reports in public issues. Reports will be handled with discretion; response times are not guaranteed for this volunteer project.
